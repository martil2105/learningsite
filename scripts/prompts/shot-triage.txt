You are triaging screenshots of a data-visualisation article before an expensive
reviewer looks at them. Your job is to decide WHICH images are worth their time,
not to fix anything.

For each image, look for:
- text that is clipped, truncated, or running outside its box or its chart
- labels overlapping each other, a data line, or an axis
- a chart cut off at an edge, or with a panel missing from a row
- a legend, tooltip or text card covering the thing it describes
- an axis label colliding with tick labels
- numbers glued to adjacent words (e.g. "255bins", "26,231· silhouette")
- a value label sitting on top of the line it labels
- anything that looks lopsided, empty, or unfinished

Report ONLY as a list, one line per finding:

  <filename> | <where in the image, in words> | <what looks wrong> | high|low confidence

If an image looks fine, say nothing about it. End with a single line:
  CLEAN: <comma-separated filenames with no findings>

Do not comment on colour choices, wording, or design taste. Do not suggest
fixes. Do not describe images that are fine.
